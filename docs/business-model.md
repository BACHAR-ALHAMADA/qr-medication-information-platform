# Business model discovery

Status: Hypotheses and research questions only. No pricing, commercial model, partnerships, revenue projections, or purchasing commitments are approved.

## Intended value

For patients, explore easier access to understandable medication information across languages and accessibility needs. For pharmacists, explore an intuitive way to support trusted information access. Potential paper reduction is conditional on legal permission, usability, and operational suitability; no cost or environmental saving is established.

The initial geographic focus is Manitoba. Pharmacies are a candidate workflow and stakeholder context; existing architecture also anticipates organizations such as manufacturers, but this does not establish them as customers or approve their workflows.

## Questions for validation (HUM-010)

- Who uses, purchases, funds, and operates the service, and who carries content review responsibilities?
- What problem is costly or frequent enough to justify adoption, and how does the service fit existing pharmacy practice?
- What ongoing costs arise from hosting, support, medical review, translation, accessibility validation, security and correction handling?
- What commercial arrangement would support those costs without compromising patient access or privacy?
- What evidence would establish useful outcomes without collecting unnecessary health information?

Do not assume patient data monetization, advertising, a subscription, or an integration is required. Discovery evidence should inform HUM-001 scope and HUM-008 operating model before commercial commitments.
