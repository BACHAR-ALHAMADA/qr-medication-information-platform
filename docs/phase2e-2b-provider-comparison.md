# Phase 2E-2B: hybrid provider evaluation comparison

Date checked: 2026-11-11 (America/Winnipeg). Status: official-document desk research; no account creation, provider connection, SDK, purchase, integration or production approval. Auth0 is the Product Owner's preferred evaluation candidate. Internal staff and pharmacy authorization remain application-owned. Trace: draft ADR-007, HUM-005/HUM-007/HUM-008, AI-002/AI-010. Product availability, limits and prices need confirmation before any commitment.

These assessments are not security certifications. For every option, compromised provider/admin credentials, misconfigured issuer/client trust, insecure linking and weak recovery can compromise authentication. No provider's organization, group, domain or email claim automatically grants application access.

## Approximate cost model, not quotes

Illustrative planning workload only: 20 pharmacies, 10 enterprise connections, 1,000 distinct local-identity MAUs and 100 distinct federated MAUs/month. Assume a qualifying new configuration/account with no other consumption of free allowances; no overlapping users. USD, before tax, CAD conversion, support, MFA/SMS/email, logs, WAF, regional differences or application engineering costs. Actual billing/MAU counting and agreements must be confirmed.

| Candidate | Observed public pricing and illustrative interpretation |
|---|---|
| Auth0 | Public cards currently show Free $0; Essentials $35/month and Professional $240/month at 500 MAUs. The page has B2C/B2B/billing selectors and warns that enterprise-connection/SSO entitlements need B2B upgrades. These numbers are NOT a hybrid B2B quote for 1,100 users/20 pharmacies/10 connections. Even the displayed organization entitlements are below this scenario; obtain an exact B2B/enterprise quote before budgeting or selecting. [Pricing](https://auth0.com/pricing). |
| Entra External ID | Core tier lists the first 50,000 MAUs free, so the illustrative workload has approximately $0 core identity charge if eligible. Premium add-ons have no free tier. The fetched page renders the paid-overage rate as a placeholder, so no numerical overage rate is asserted. Region/currency, MFA, add-ons and support require confirmation. [Official price page](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/microsoft-entra-external-id/). |
| Cognito | Essentials/Lite currently include 10,000 local/social MAUs free; SAML/OIDC has a separate 50-MAU free allowance, then $0.015/federated MAU. Under the stated assumptions, Essentials base would be about `(100-50) x $0.015 = $0.75/month`; this excludes communication/security/support costs. Plus has no local free allowance and its shown local rate is $0.020/MAU, yielding roughly $20.75 for the same split before extras. Do not infer equal MFA/risk/logging capability or total operating cost from these base numbers. [Pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/cognito/pricing/). |

Quotes should cover growth scenarios separately: local MAUs, federated MAUs, pharmacy count, enterprise connection count, shared versus dedicated connections, MFA/step-up, recovery, logs/retention, development environments, SLA/support, custom domains and region. Multiple provider identities for one internal employee may affect provider billing. Free-tier eligibility is not an implementation or production-readiness decision.

## Data residency and privacy questions

- Auth0 lists Canada among public cloud deployment regions; verify selected plan/tenant locality, backups, logs, support access, subprocessors and cross-border processing rather than treating the region label as a complete guarantee. [Deployment](https://auth0.com/platform/cloud-deployment).
- Entra External ID offers a tenant storage-location choice, with availability/locations differing from workforce Entra and some options tied to Go-Local. Confirm the actual external-tenant geography and applicable feature exceptions; choosing a country must not be assumed to guarantee Canadian-only processing. [Microsoft residency documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/fundamentals/data-residency).
- Cognito stores user profiles in the user pool's region, but optional features can send data elsewhere; mail/SMS/analytics settings need review. Canada Central is listed as a service endpoint. [Regional considerations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cognito/latest/developerguide/security-cognito-regional-data-considerations.html), [service regions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/general/latest/gr/cognito.html).

For all three: verify region availability in the chosen tier, profile/credential/log/backup locations, disaster recovery, support and administration access, deletion/export, breach notification, contract/DPA and subprocessors. HUM-007 requires qualified review of actual applicability; no Manitoba/Canadian legal conclusion or approved residency obligation is invented here. Only synthetic research material is used.

## Recommendation and evaluation gates

Evaluate Auth0 first, preserving the approved preference. Require proof that local credentials and enterprise federation coexist without email autolinking, exact broker identities remain stable, controlled dual-proof linking is possible, MFA provenance can be interpreted, A's enterprise IdP cannot authenticate into B without policy permission, and application membership remains authoritative. Confirm the example's organization/connection entitlements and total cost. Entra External ID is the comparison candidate for enterprise-heavy adoption; Cognito is the price/control comparison with additional B2B workflow ownership.

This is a recommendation for an evaluation order only. Provider/model selection, account creation, commercial discussions, real sandbox integration, region and budget still need explicit authorization. The next [Phase 2E-2C persistence proposal](phase2e-2b-identity-mapping-design.md#narrow-phase-2e-2c-proposal-and-approvals) requires none of these provider SDKs or accounts.
